# Critical gaps and next steps

## Must be completed before the methods are final

1. **Boundary and catchments:** obtain a traceable Pantaron boundary, authoritative hydrography, final outlets, and two to four selected complete catchments.
2. **Management evidence:** obtain official plans and intervention records identifying responsible institutions, locations, dates, implementation, and monitoring.
3. **Pilot:** test image availability, class separability, DEM drainage, rainfall support, and feasible field access in two candidate catchments.
4. **Validation design:** calculate the land-cover reference sample, keep it independent from training data, and prepare field forms, permissions, safety, and instrument calibration.
5. **Water question:** decide whether the field component addresses stream condition, discharge, water quality, or a limited combination. One visit cannot represent seasonal condition.
6. **Erosion method:** use susceptibility screening unless defensible RUSLE R, K, LS, C, and P factors plus evaluation evidence are available.
7. **Management effectiveness:** narrow this to management-response coverage unless a before–after or comparison design is feasible.
8. **Ethics and governance:** secure clearance before interviews; protect sensitive Indigenous/community locations and knowledge.
9. **Literature:** fully appraise the strongest records from the refreshed 159-record discovery table. Metadata and RSS items remain discovery aids.

## Defense message

The contribution is not “many satellite maps.” It is a transparent evaluation linking land condition, water condition, management response, validation, and uncertainty within defensible catchments. Recommendations will identify what to protect, restore, manage, monitor, or investigate and why.
